# Studio HQ UI audit (14 Nov 2026)

Screenshots: `docs/design/audit/before/` (all 14 views in Defied, Pearl and Concrete, 1440×900, taken from a copy of the library).

## What is wrong

**Shell**
- Three stacked navigation layers: icon rail, then a tab bar, then filter pills inside the page. You need two clicks to reach most screens and the rail labels (Make, Sell, Reach) do not tell you what is inside.
- The rail is 36 px of icons with tiny caps labels. Hit areas are small, active state is just a box.
- The floating player is a full-width bar that is always on, even when nothing plays ("Nothing playing 0:00"). It eats 90 px of every view.
- The settings and assistant icons sit loose at the bottom of the rail with no label.

**Hierarchy**
- Every page opens with a giant centred hero title ("GOOD AFTERNOON.", "BEATS", "ALL LEADS", "PITCH CRM") that takes 25 to 30 % of the screen and says nothing the tab bar did not already say.
- Home buries the real content: "Next up" rows sit under the greeting and the ticker; the cards (Catalog health, Studio session, Streak) are below the fold.
- The ticker on Home and Pitch CRM repeats the same numbers, and on Pitch CRM it is duplicated across the top AND as four big stat boxes.

**Density and layout**
- Beats: a 30 % wide empty "Select a beat" inspector is permanently open; the card grid shows only 8 beats per screen, and the cards are the same black square with a waveform and a letter. No cover art, no way to tell beats apart at a glance.
- Leads: good density, but 5 rows of filters (browse, genre, works-with, view pills, genre pills) before the first lead.
- Pitch CRM: contact list in a narrow column, and the main area is mostly empty stat boxes reading 0.
- Centred layouts everywhere (Home, Beats header) fight left-aligned lists below them.

**Components**
- Pills are used for everything: tabs, filters, statuses, buttons, view switches. Nothing tells you which pill is an action and which is a filter.
- Status badges, genre chips and tab pills share one outline style.
- Concrete's hazard stripe sits on top of the player and cuts across content.

**Type**
- Defied's chrome display face is used for page titles at a size that dominates; body text is 11 to 12 px greys with low contrast on black.
- Tiny tracked-out caps for labels (Next up, ticker labels) are hard to read.

**States**
- Empty states are a single grey line ("Nothing due. You're caught up.", "Select a beat").
- Loading and error states are not designed.

**Bugs seen**
- Pitch CRM in Pearl: the ticker renders twice side by side at the top.
- Beats card grid uses the first letter of the title as the "art", so State Of Anarchy and Spider look the same.
