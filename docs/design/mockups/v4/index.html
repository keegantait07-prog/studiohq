Three directions for Studio HQ v4

Each one covers the shell, Home, a dense view (Beats) and a dialog (Pitch). Each can be flipped between Defied and Concrete with the control in the corner, to show that the themes stay their own worlds on the new component system. Content is your real library: beat names, BPM, keys, counts, leads.

A · Deck

The app is an instrument. The player is the header, sections sit in one flat strip, Home shows every beat on a line from Idea to Sold.

Navigation
Transport bar + one section strip with a gliding underline. No sidebar.
Home
One sentence telling you what to do, the Idea→Sold line, four solid colour blocks.
Beats
Dense table with waveforms and a sticky inspector.
Pitch
A routing grid: beats across the top, people down the side, tick who gets what.

B · Crate

Your catalogue as physical records. Every beat gets a printed sleeve made from its own name. Home puts one beat on the table.

Navigation
A typographic index on the left: words and counts, no icons.
Home
One record on the table, its liner notes, what is next on the pile.
Beats
A wall of sleeves with stamped statuses; details slide over as a sheet.
Pitch
An envelope: the sleeves you are sending, address labels, the letter.
21st refs
Solenne 1290, Tweenly KPI 39900 (halftone), Curated Recommendations 1392, Toast Gravity 33302, Dashboard Shell 43212

C · Console

Command-first. One line at the top does everything, Home is a numbered queue, a Now rail keeps the player and numbers in view.

Navigation
⌘K command line + a small dock at the bottom. No sidebar.
Home
Your queue, ordered by what gets a beat closest to money; "Since you left" feed.
Beats
A board you drag beats across.
Pitch
Type it: "pitch spider to south", preview, review emails.

How they differ

RiskCan read as a spreadsheet if themes stay flatCovers are generated; weaker for 400+ loopsHides sections; relies on you using ⌘K

What stays the same in all three

Same token layer (semantic names copied from how 21st themes are built), same motion rules (80/180/320 ms, ease-out on enter, no animation on keyboard row moves, reduced motion respected), same data, same features. The giant page titles, the three stacked nav layers and the always-on empty player bar are gone in all three.
