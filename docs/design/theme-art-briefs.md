# Theme art briefs

The exact prompts sent to Gemini (scripts/nanobanana.py) for the theme backdrops and concepts, so any image can be regenerated or extended.
Written to the image-prompting guide (prose, subject, composition, lighting, imperfections, hard exclusions) and the art-direction notes
(every image states its form, content and context). Backdrops used the higher-detail model at 2K; concepts used the standard model at 3:2.

## What makes these read as photographs, not generated art
- A named camera, lens, aperture, film stock or exposure, so the model reaches for photographic rendering.
- One composition decision with asymmetry (subject on a third, open space in the centre for the interface).
- One light source, described by direction, colour and quality.
- Explicit imperfections: duckweed, dust, grain, halation, a tilted horizon, ash on the lens.
- One colour tension (orange koi on teal black, one red sign on slate, lava on black).
- No glow, no stylisation, no readable text.

## Backdrops (16:9, 2K)

### Koi Pond
Form: low-light handheld photograph. Content: koi, moss lantern, bridge, one ripple. Context: calm backdrop with the centre left open for the list.
> A photograph shot on a full-frame mirrorless camera with a 35mm f/1.4 lens at f/2.2, handheld, ISO 1600, in a private temple garden in Kyoto twenty minutes after sunset. Composition: camera low, just above the water. A weathered stone tsukubai basin and a moss-covered stone lantern sit on the left third; a single arched wooden bridge is softly out of focus at upper right; the centre of the frame is open dark water. Subject: three nishikigoi, two red-and-white kohaku and one dark sumi, drifting close to the surface with irregular, asymmetric markings; one koi has just broken the surface with its lip, making a single small ripple. Lighting: the last cool blue light from the sky, plus one distant paper lantern giving a faint amber reflection on the water. Imperfections: patches of duckweed, two fallen maple leaves, algae on the stone, visible sensor noise, slightly uneven exposure and a gentle vignette. Colour: restrained deep teal and ink black, with the orange koi as the single warm tension. Honest and unposed, no glow, no stylisation, no text.

### City Pop
Form: pushed 35mm film street photograph. Content: wet dusk street, vending machine, blossom. Context: a pink-and-slate backdrop. Known issues: horizon tilted about 20 degrees, and the convenience-store sign resembles a real brand logo.
> A photograph on 35mm Kodak Portra 400 film pushed one stop, a casual compact-camera street shot of a residential street beside a river in Kyoto at 6:40 pm in early April, just after rain. Composition: eye level with the camera at the kerb. A leaning concrete utility pole and tangled power lines cut through the right third; a cherry blossom branch intrudes out of focus from the top left; the wet street recedes to a single white-lit vending machine; a small parked kei truck sits at the edge. Lighting: a dusk gradient from peach to blue-grey; the vending machine and one convenience-store sign are the only artificial lights, reflecting in long soft streaks on the wet asphalt with the sky's pink and slate. Imperfections: visible film grain, faint halation around the lights, one dust speck, a slightly tilted horizon. Colour: faded pink, slate blue and one red sign as the only strong note. No people, no readable text, no stylisation.

### Magma
Form: long-exposure night photograph. Content: lava crust and a weathered sheep skull. Context: dark backdrop with the glow at the left.
> A photograph on a full-frame DSLR with a 20mm f/1.8 lens, tripod, 8 second exposure at ISO 800, at the edge of an active lava field in Iceland two hours after midnight. Composition: camera 30 centimetres above black basalt gravel. A weathered sheep skull, half buried in volcanic ash, sits in the lower right third catching orange rim light. A cracked lava crust glows through the middle distance, with steam and gas rising to a star-filled sky at the top. Lighting: only the glow of the lava, a deep orange, lighting the skull and gravel from behind and from the left, with cold starlight as a faint fill. Imperfections: ash dust on the lens, smoke haze softening the horizon, a few hot pixels, one trail of sparks. Colour: black, burnt umber and incandescent orange, with no blue except the sky. Real texture and scale, no equipment in frame, no text.

## New theme concepts (3:2, interface mockups)
Each states the interface text it may contain, to keep the generator from inventing gibberish. Each theme changes structure, not only colour.

- **Yatai** (ramen cart at night): the list is a hand-brushed menu board, tempo sits in the price column, status is a red hanko stamp. Real wood, washi, rain, steam.
- **Shoji Snow** (light): the window is a wall of washi paper panels with cedar lattice for dividers and one clear pane onto falling snow; brush-ink titles; hanko stamps.
- **Xerox Zine** (light): halftone photographs, torn paper, masking tape, typed index cards per beat, fluorescent orange stamps, hard offset shadows, slightly rotated cards.
- **Cassette Lab** (warm beige): scuffed hardware, a mechanical tape counter, embossed label-maker strips for status, beats as J-cards in a rack, VU needle.
- **Abyss**: a brass porthole onto bioluminescent jellyfish and marine snow, riveted steel panels, depth gauge and sonar screen with beats as contacts.
